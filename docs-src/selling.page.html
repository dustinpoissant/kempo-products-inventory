Selling takes the materials

When a sale is added to kempo-products' ledger (an entry that takes quantity from a linked product), this extension takes every material the product uses out of inventory in one atomic call. Either all of them are taken or none are, so a sale never takes the resin and then finds the paint short.

  • The amount is the per-unit amount times the quantity sold, for the materials that match the choices sold (the line's options).
  • Every inventory movement carries the order reference, written into its note, so you can find what an order took.
  • An entry is added once per reference, so adding the same one again does not take anything twice. If any material is short the entry is refused and nothing is taken.

Quantity follows the materials

A linked product's quantity is how many can be made from what is on hand: for each material, the quantity in inventory divided by the amount per unit, rounded down, and the smallest of those numbers wins. The scarcest material limits the product.

The Product Quantity page lists every linked product and what limits it.

Choices are worked out the same way: a colour is marked sold out when there is not enough of the materials it needs for one more.

Products that share materials

Two products can use the same material, and both stay correct. With 300 g of PLA and two products that each need 200 g, each shows 1 in stock. Sell one and 100 g is left, so both go out of stock, because neither can be made any more.

Restocking

Receiving resin in the inventory, or correcting a count, recalculates every product made from that item straight away. A product that was out of stock comes back, with no action in the products admin.

Putting a sale back

When an entry that puts a sale back is added (a refund or a cancelled order: undoes is the sale's reference), exactly what the sale took is put back. The extension remembers the amounts it took for each reference, so it is correct even if the links were edited since. Putting back twice puts back once. Adding to a linked product without saying which sale it undoes is refused, because its quantity follows the materials.
