When it happens

Normally nothing goes wrong, because a product refuses to sell more than the materials allow. But if stock was changed behind the system's back, for instance directly in the database, the materials for a purchase may not be there.

The purchase stands, because it already happened. The extension records a problem with the order reference and what was short, and recalculates the affected products so they show what is really left.

The Product Stock page

Open Product Stock in the admin menu to see what is linked and what needs attention.

The Product Stock page.

Each problem can be dismissed once you have dealt with it.

Deleting an inventory item

An inventory item that products are made from cannot be deleted until it is removed from them. The deletion is refused with a message naming the products, so a product is never left depending on something that no longer exists. Deleting a product forgets what it was made from.

Repairing

Recalculate everything on the Product Stock page is the repair tool: it works out every linked product's stock again from the inventory as it is now. Use it after changing the database directly, or after restoring a backup.
