Kempo Products Inventory

GitHub npm

Sell a model car and the resin, paint and filament it is made from come out of stock automatically.

This connects kempo-products to kempo-inventory. Neither of those needs the other: a catalog can exist with no inventory, and an inventory with no products. Install this one when you use both.

The Made from tab on a product's form.

What it does

You tell it what a product is made from. Open a product and use its Made from tab:

ProductMaterialPer unitUsed
Model carBlack resin175always
Model carTPU filament12always
Model carRed paint20only with Color: Red
Model carBlue paint20only with Color: Blue
KeychainBlack resin35always

Then, with no further setup:

  • Selling takes the materials. When a purchase is recorded, 175 g of resin, 12 g of TPU and 20 ml of the colour that was bought are taken from inventory, all together or not at all. Each inventory movement carries the order reference.
  • Stock follows the materials. Each product's stock is how many can be made from what is on hand, limited by the scarcest material. A colour is marked sold out when there is not enough of its paint for one more.
  • Products that share materials stay honest. With 300 g of PLA and two products that each need 200 g, selling one leaves 100 g and both go out of stock.
  • Restocking brings them back. Receiving resin, or correcting a count, updates every product made from it straight away.
  • Reversing a purchase puts back exactly what it took, even if the materials were edited since.

Install

npm install kempo-products-inventory

Then install it from Admin > Extensions. Kempo refuses to enable it until kempo-products and kempo-inventory are both enabled. There is nothing to configure and it adds no permissions: to link materials you need products:update and kempo-inventory:items:read.

Quick start

1. Have some materials

In Inventory, add the items your products are made from, with their stock in whole units: grams and millilitres, not kilograms and litres.

2. Have a product

In Products, add the product you sell, with its price and options.

3. Say what it is made from

Open the product, find the Made from tab, add each material and how much one unit uses, and save. The product's stock is now worked out for you. See Made from.

4. Sell one

When a purchase is recorded, the materials are taken out of inventory. Look at the item's history in the inventory admin to see the movement, with the order reference.

How it fits together

It reaches the other extensions only through their SDKs, and adds its interface through fragments those extensions provide.

ExtensionWhat this uses from it
kempo-productsThe purchase events, setStock, setManagedBy and setChoiceAvailability, and the products-admin-product-tabs location on a product's form
kempo-inventoryThe stock event, the adjustStockMany call that takes several materials atomically, and the panel fragment on an item's page

The three pieces of interface it adds are the Made from tab on a product's form, Make product from this item on an inventory item's page, and a Product Stock page. See the reference for every event it listens to.
